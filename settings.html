A motor takes its worst check: it is Good only when every check is Good, and Not OK as soon as any one check reaches Not OK. So a motor can be Not OK below the temperature limit if its rise above ambient is too high. A motor also shows Monitor when its predicted final rise will reach the Not OK limit, and Not OK when a terminal goes outside the limits below.
