Motor conditions

Set what counts as Stable, Processing and Unstable · applies to every motor as soon as you save
Unsaved changes

A motor takes its worst check. It is Unstable as soon as any check reaches its Unstable limit, or a terminal goes outside the limits below. It is Stable only when its temperatures have settled and every check is below its Processing limit. Otherwise it is Processing: still heating, or between the two limits. A motor also shows Processing when its predicted final rise will reach the Unstable limit.
